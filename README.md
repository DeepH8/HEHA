HEHA

This game is inspired by Roblox obbies. Player tries to reach the end, avoiding death. If the player dies 10 times before reaching the end, they lose.

Move(walk-jump-run) > Die > Last checkpoint > Move > Reach checkpoint > Harder obstacles > End/Win

Player has a route ahead of them to reach the end, adn they should not die from obstacles and by falling. If the player dies 10 times before reaching the end, they lose and start from the beginning.
